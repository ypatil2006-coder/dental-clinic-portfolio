## Direction contract

THESIS: One dentist, presented as the author of the work. The page refuses the multi-practitioner clinic brochure — no team grid, no facility tour, no service cards — and behaves like a solo designer's portfolio: person first, work second, one action throughout.

OWN-WORLD: Near-black aubergine ground a hair off black, signal red as the only saturated voice, warm off-white type, hairline rules, and one tightly-set condensed display face against a small neutral grotesk for everything functional. The language is oversized lettering, red fields, and thin lines — recognizable with all content removed.

STORY: The visitor lands on a person, not an institution. They read one line of intent, see a small set of chosen cases, meet the practitioner in their own words, and end at a single WhatsApp action with a prepared message. Nothing else competes for the tap.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Small fixed top bar with name-mark left and three text links right. Below it, a monumental display line fills most of the width, with a single supporting sentence beneath. A red field enters under the type as the scroll begins. The WhatsApp button sits in the top bar and repeats at the close; on phones it becomes a sticky bottom bar.

FORM: pinned — the user's words: "just try mimic the website as much as possible". seed: none (pinned). The reference is melon.studio: dark editorial portfolio, huge type, red accent fields, scroll-linked light/dark shifts, masked line reveals, hover image/type swaps.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
